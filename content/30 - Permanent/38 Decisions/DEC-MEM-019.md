# DEC — Scope is five stages of increasing complexity: stages 1-2 (triage,…

Scope is five stages of increasing complexity: stages 1-2 (triage, estimation) are the guaranteed MVP; stage 3 (pairwise prioritization) is desirable; stages 4 (retrieval-augmented) and 5 (multi-agent PMO) are aspirational and explicitly out of MVP scope.
