# DEC — A known limitation is imprecise GPU energy attribution via the local…

A known limitation is imprecise GPU energy attribution via the local inference server; emissions are therefore reported as relative measures between conditions rather than absolute calibrated values, with physical-measurement calibration deferred to future work.
