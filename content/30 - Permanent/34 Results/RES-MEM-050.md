# RES — Small models lead in raw efficiency (quality per gram CO2) for triage and…

Small models lead in raw efficiency (quality per gram CO2) for triage and estimation while large models lead in absolute quality at disproportionate energy cost; no single model dominates all dimensions, so model choice should be per-task, not per-deployment.
