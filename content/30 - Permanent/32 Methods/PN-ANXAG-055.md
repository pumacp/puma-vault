# PN — PUMA Community operates as a serverless voluntary federation

PUMA Community operates as a serverless voluntary federation: all infrastructure on free GitHub services (Actions, Pages, Wiki, Discussions) plus HF Spaces and Zenodo, with no server, database, or recurring cost, so a finished project needs no perpetual maintenance.
