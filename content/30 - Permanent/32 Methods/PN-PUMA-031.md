# PN — PUMA treats inference compute cost as a first-class reported metric…

PUMA treats inference compute cost as a first-class reported metric (motivated by Strubell et al. 2019), reporting energy and carbon alongside F1/MAE so a marginally-more-accurate but far more energy-hungry model is not assumed better for deployment.
