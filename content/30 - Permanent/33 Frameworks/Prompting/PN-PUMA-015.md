# PN — PUMA supports multi-strategy prompting

PUMA supports multi-strategy prompting: zero-shot, zero-shot-CoT, few-shot (k=3/5/8), CoT few-shot, RCOIF, contextual anchoring, EGI, and self-consistency, implemented as 11 strategy classes behind a Strategy ABC.
