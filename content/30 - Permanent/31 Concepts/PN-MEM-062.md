# PN — The title question is answered cautiously

The title question is answered cautiously: language models can assist concrete bounded tasks (triage, estimation, prioritization) with measurable above-baseline quality and competitive energy cost, but cannot manage projects in the full sense, so project management remains an essentially human practice that AI can augment but not replace.
