# PN — Meeting the estimation MVP threshold and the H2 hypothesis contrast are…

Meeting the estimation MVP threshold and the H2 hypothesis contrast are independent: the estimation MVP is functionally valid even though H2 is not statistically sustained, because product thresholds and inferential significance answer different questions.
