# PN — PUMA occupies a deliberately narrow niche

PUMA occupies a deliberately narrow niche: a comparative-evaluation platform for local LLM agents that is reproducible by design, comparative by construction, and carbon-aware by instrumentation, avoiding broader general-purpose benchmarks or fully automated PMO platforms to guarantee methodological depth.
