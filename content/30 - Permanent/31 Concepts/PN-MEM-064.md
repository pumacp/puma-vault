# PN — The H2 non-rejection is reframed as a valuable methodological finding…

The H2 non-rejection is reframed as a valuable methodological finding rather than a failure: under Popper's criterion H2 was refutable and the evidence did not sustain it, demonstrating the rigor of the falsifiable design.
