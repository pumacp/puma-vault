# PN — Technology project management in multi-project organizations suffers a…

Technology project management in multi-project organizations suffers a structural problem: information needed to detect risk, prioritize work and anticipate deviations is scattered across heterogeneous tools, making manual review slow and dependent on individual cognitive capacity.
