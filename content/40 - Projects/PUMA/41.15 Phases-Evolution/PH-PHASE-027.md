# PH — The design phase reaffirmed scope boundaries as deliberate exclusions

The design phase reaffirmed scope boundaries as deliberate exclusions: no proprietary-API model evaluation (local-only), no fine-tuning (out-of-the-box capabilities only), no replacement of human PMO decisions, and no legal RGPD/AI-Act guarantees beyond privacy-by-design via local execution.
