## Pass 1 Notes (Preliminary)

### The Containment Problem

Suleiman argues that two forces are in tension:
1. **Incentives to proliferate**: Commercial pressure, national competition, democratization arguments
2. **Need to contain**: Safety, concentration of power, displacement risks

For PM AI tools like PUMA:
- **Proliferation pressure**: Every company wants a cheaper, faster issue triage pipeline
- **Containment need**: Unconstrained AI routing of work affects developer careers, workload equity, organizational power

### Relevance to PUMA's Ethics Chapter

1. **Power concentration risk**: If PUMA-like tools are only affordable for large tech companies, smaller organizations are disadvantaged in PM efficiency
2. **Accountability gap**: Who is responsible when an AI-triaged sprint fails? (→ HITL design)
3. **Displacement thesis**: PUMA's automation of PM workflows contributes to the broader labor displacement pattern Suleiman documents
